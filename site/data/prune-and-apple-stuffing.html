Prune and Apple Stuffing For Goose

Ingredients

24 large prunes   
Salt, pepper
6 cooked apples   
Herbs
Brandy

Method

Soak the prunes in brandy overnight. Stone then and chop them into four. Peel, core and quarter the apples and chop the quarters in half again.
Mix up with the herbs, salt and pepper and a little of the remaining brandy to moisten.

Stuff the goose with the mixture.
