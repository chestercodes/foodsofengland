Apple puree, moulded, "or pile them in the centre of your dish and pour a custard over them." (Walsh 1859)

Original Receipt from 'The English Cookery Book' edited by JH Walsh Walsh 1859;

Apple And Custard.
1039. Prepare apples as for sauce only stiffer, allow them to remain till cold. Put them into a simple mould to give a shape to them, or pile them in the centre of your dish and pour a custard over them.
