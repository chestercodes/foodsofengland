Fried potato rounds, with melted butter, sack and sugar poured over, "These are a pretty corner-plate" (Glasse 1747)

Original Receipt in 'The Art of Cookery, Made Plain and Easy' by Hannah Glasse, 1747 (Glasse 1747);

To fry potatoes
CUT them into thin slices, as big as a crown piece, fry them brown, lay them in the plate or dish, pour Melted Butter, and sack and sugar over them. These are a pretty corner-plate.
