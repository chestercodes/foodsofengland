Original Receipt in the 15th Century 'Austin Manuscripts' (Austin 1440)

Gaylede. Take almond milk & flour of rice, & do thereto sugar or hony, & powder ginger & galyngale; then take figys, an kerve them a-to, or raysonys y-hole, or hard wastel y-diced and coloure it with saunderys, & seethe it & dresse them yn.
