Warm ale with cloves, sweet spices, apple and sugar.


Widdicombe Fair used to advertise ginger wine, c1930
Image: Unknown


The original source of this receipt isn't known. Can you help? editor@foodsofengland.co.uk

2 pints of ale
5 cloves
1 large red eating apple
large pinch of ground mixed spice
large pinch of ground nutmeg
3 level teaspoon sugar

Heat the ale gently, without boiling. Stir in the spices and sugar. Slice the apple and float in the drink just before serving hot.
