Stoned dates stuffed with marzipan and presented in tiny paper cases. A Christmas treat.

Original Receipt in the 'Taunton Courier, and Western Advertiser' - Wednesday 21 January 1931

MARZIPAN DATES.
Remove the stones from some dates, and fill in the centres with a little of the almond paste rolled into an oblong shape. Press together again, and roll in icing sugar.
