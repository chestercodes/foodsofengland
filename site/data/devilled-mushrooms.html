Mushrooms fried with hot spices.

The original source of this receipt isn't known. Can you help? editor@foodsofengland.co.uk

1 oz Butter
6 oz Mushrooms, sliced
1 Tablespoon Plain flour
1/4 pint Milk
2 Teaspoons Worcester sauce
1 Teaspoon mustard
4 Slices Hot buttered toast

Method
Melt the butter in a saucepan, saute the mushrooms for 2 minutes. Add the flour, cook for a minute and slowly add the remaining ingredients, stirring continuously until the mixture boils. Simmer for 2 minutes. Pile on top of hot toast, serve immediately garnished with parsley.
