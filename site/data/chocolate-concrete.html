CHOCOLATE CONCRETE

8oz Plain flour
2 oz cocoa (or drinking chocolate)
4 oz sugar
4 oz melted butter

Mix all dry ingredients together
Add melted butter and mix together
Put into a 7" greased tin and cook - gas mark 3 for approx 45 mins.
Traditionaly served with Pink Custard
