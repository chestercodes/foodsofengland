Chopped parsley with vinegar, breadcrumbs and salt (Austin 1440)

Original Receipt in the 15th Century 'Austin Manuscripts' (Austin 1440)

Sauce percely. Take percely, and grynde hit with vynegre & a litel brede and salt, and strayne it thurg a straynour, and serue it forthe.
