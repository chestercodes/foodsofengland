Mushroom, onion, basil, white Lancashire cheese and breadcrumbs wrapped in very thin plain shortcrust pastry or in flatted white bread, baked.

4 oz sliced mushrooms
1 onion, finely chopped
1 tsp dried basil
1 ½ oz butter
4 oz fresh breadcrumbs
1/4 lb Lancashire cheese

Gently fry the mushrooms and onions until soft. Add the breadcrumbs, basil and crumbled cheese and stir together. Roll 6 slices of white bread flat and spread with the mushroom mixture. Roll each slice up, place on a greased baking tray and bake for 5 to 15 minutes at 190C 375F until brown.
